Pipeline

Where the programs in the 5 October 2026 review sat. “Investigational” means a program is being studied. It does not mean it works.

Not medical advice. This board is a snapshot, last reviewed 5 October 2026. It does not rewrite itself when a headline appears. Live recruiting status is on Open trials. The narrative, with the same sources, is on Where things stand.

Last reviewed 5 October 2026

Preclinical

Cure Rare Disease ASO

Splice-switching antisense concept. Preclinical, preparing an IND. FDA orphan designation in May 2025. No SCA3 treatment trial was posted at this review.

For someone without symptoms: not in the clinic.

AAV / RNAi and other gene therapy

Viral or RNA approaches meant to silence ATXN3. Preclinical in the materials reviewed. Not in a human SCA3 trial as of this page.

For someone without symptoms: no.

Phase 1

VO659 (Vico)

Antisense oligonucleotide given into the spinal fluid, aimed at expanded CAG RNA. SCA1, SCA3, and Huntington disease. Listed as Phase 1/2a.

At review: recruiting in Europe. NCT05822908. Ages 25–60, mild to moderate ataxia. A U.S. study was described by the company in February 2026 as cleared. Treat that as a company update.

For someone without symptoms: no.

S233107 (Servier)

Intrathecal drug designed to lower faulty ataxin-3. Phase 1b/2a, randomized, placebo-controlled. Combined early studies are shown in this column.

At review: not yet recruiting. Europe first. EU trial 2025-525111-17-00. No NCT number had been posted. Sponsor page.

For someone without symptoms: no. Adults with symptomatic SCA3.

Phase 2

No program in the 5 October 2026 review was in a standalone Phase 2. VO659 and S233107 are combined early-phase studies and are listed under Phase 1.

Phase 3

No program in that review was still in Phase 3. Troriluzole had finished Phase 3 and is under Stopped.

Regulatory review

Stemchymal

Donor mesenchymal stromal cells. Not gene silencing. A marketing application was submitted in Japan in June 2026 for SCA3 and SCA6.

That filing is not an FDA or EMA approval, and it is not approval in Japan unless a regulator grants it.

Company release, 24 June 2026.

Approved

None. As of this review there is no approved drug that prevents SCA3, cures it, or has been shown to slow the underlying nerve-cell damage.

Stopped

BIIB132 (Biogen)

Antisense drug against ATXN3. Phase 1 terminated in 2023, sponsor decision.

NCT05160558.

Troriluzole (Biohaven)

Oral drug that modulates glutamate. Not a gene-silencing drug. Phase 3 completed. The U.S. FDA issued a Complete Response Letter in November 2025. That is a rejection of the application, not an approval.

NCT03701399. Company release.

What this board leaves out

Device studies and stem-cell studies that were not in the review table are not given a stage here. Two of them, temporal interference stimulation (NCT07743866) and cerebellar deep-brain stimulation (NCT07752095), were not yet recruiting at the review. They are not gene silencing. If a registry still lists them as open, they appear on Open trials with the registry’s own words.
