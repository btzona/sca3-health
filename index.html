Ataxin-3

Papers, trials, and news on spinocerebellar ataxia type 3, also called Machado-Joseph disease.

A note from Bryan Timmins

The mother of my two daughters died of SCA3 at age 42. Each of our daughters now has a 50/50 chance of developing the same disease. I made this site primarily for them, and also for anyone who wants to track the latest research and developments on this specific form of ataxia.

Bryan Timmins
