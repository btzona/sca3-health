In the clinic, or at a regulator

Vico Therapeutics

Private

Developing
VO659 for SCA3, and also for SCA1 and Huntington’s disease.
Modality
Antisense oligonucleotide. It is given into the spinal fluid and is designed to bind the expanded CAG repeat in the disease RNA.
Stage
Phase 1/2a.
Status
ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT05822908 as recruiting. The registry’s last update was posted 19 August 2026. On 24 February 2026 the company said the U.S. FDA had cleared an IND and that it planned a U.S. trial later in 2026. No separate U.S. trial number was found for this page.

REPROCELL

Public

Developing
Stemchymal for SCA3 and SCA6. REPROCELL holds the Japan commercialization rights. Steminent Biotherapeutics, in Taiwan, is the manufacturer named in the application.
Modality
Cell therapy. Donor mesenchymal stem cells, not a gene-silencing drug. The application asks to use it to slow the progression of ataxia.
Stage
Marketing application in Japan. This is not an approval.
Status
REPROCELL said on 24 June 2026 that it had submitted the application. The product had orphan regenerative-medicine designation in Japan in December 2018, which makes it eligible for a priority review. Eligibility for review is not a decision. No FDA or EMA approval is claimed here.

No ClinicalTrials.gov or EU CTIS record is listed for this program.

Company release, 24 June 2026 · Product page

No ClinicalTrials.gov or EU trial number was found for the Japan application itself.

Private

Developing
19#iSCLife, also written SCLnow 19#, for spinocerebellar ataxia types 1, 2, 3, and 6.

Cure Rare Disease

Nonprofit

Developing
CRD-002 for SCA3.
Modality
Antisense oligonucleotide. The company describes it as a single strand aimed at the cause of SCA3.
Stage
Preclinical. IND-enabling work, as the company described it in May 2025.
Status
The FDA granted orphan-drug designation, announced 14 May 2025. The same announcement said the group was on track to finish IND-enabling studies and submit an IND in 2026. No SCA3 treatment trial from this group was found on a registry for this page, and a later confirmation that the IND was filed was not found.

No ClinicalTrials.gov or EU CTIS record is listed for this program.

Neurolixis

Private

Developing
NLX-112, also called befiradol. The orphan designations are for spinocerebellar ataxia, not for SCA3 alone.
Modality
Small-molecule drug. A serotonin 5-HT1A receptor agonist. It is not a gene-silencing or cell therapy.
Stage
Preclinical for SCA3. Published mouse studies in 2025. No SCA3 clinical trial was found.
Status
EMA orphan designation EU/3/24/2951 on 25 July 2024, for befiradol fumarate in spinocerebellar ataxia. FDA orphan designation on 15 May 2025, sponsor Neurolixis, Inc., for the same broad use. On 24 January 2025 the company called NLX-112 Phase 2-ready and pointed to earlier human safety data and a Parkinson’s study. Those are not an SCA3 trial.

No ClinicalTrials.gov or EU CTIS record is listed for this program.

Company news, 24 January 2025 · EMA orphan designation · FDA orphan designation

Sarepta Therapeutics

Public

Developing
SRP-1006 for SCA3. The program is marked external on Sarepta’s page. Arrowhead’s 2024 collaboration release named an ATXN3 siRNA candidate, ARO-ATXN3, among the preclinical programs licensed to Sarepta.
Modality
siRNA, a form of RNA interference aimed at ATXN3.
Stage
Discovery / preclinical, on Sarepta’s program page.
Status
No SCA3 trial registry entry was found. Arrowhead said in November 2024 that it expected the ATXN3 candidate to be ready for a clinical trial application in 2026. Sarepta’s page, read for this update, still places SRP-1006 in discovery/preclinical.

No ClinicalTrials.gov or EU CTIS record is listed for this program.

Servier

Foundation-owned

Developing
S233107 for SCA3. Servier says it is governed by the non-profit Fondation Internationale de Recherche Servier. It is not a listed company, so there is no ticker.
Modality
Intrathecal solution for injection, 15 mg/mL, in the EU trial record. The sponsor page says it is designed to reduce faulty ataxin-3. That page does not name the chemical class, so this card does not either.
Stage
Phase 1b/2a first-in-human trial. The application was authorised in late July and early August 2026.
Status
The public CTIS page for 2025-525111-17-00, read 17 October 2026, lists the overall trial status as end of trial on 11 September 2026. Denmark, France, Spain, and Portugal are each marked Ended, with the reason Sponsor Decision. The recruitment start and end columns on that page are blank. The same page still prints the application’s estimated recruitment start of 1 September 2026 and estimated end of 31 October 2030. Servier’s own trial page, read the same day, still says not yet recruiting and says centres in the US and Japan will be added. Open trials follows the CTIS record, so this study stays off that list.

Biohaven

Public

Developing
Troriluzole, also called VYGLXIA and BHV-4157, for spinocerebellar ataxia. The application and the trial cover SCA3 along with other SCA types. It is not an SCA3-only gene therapy.
Modality
Oral small molecule. A prodrug of riluzole, meant to modulate glutamate. Not gene silencing.
Stage
Phase 3 study completed its blinded treatment. A marketing application was filed in the United States.
Status
The FDA issued a complete response letter on 4 November 2025 for NDA 210862. That letter says the application did not show substantial evidence of effectiveness, and it is not an approval. The company said that day it would ask for a meeting about a future application. ClinicalTrials.gov still listed NCT03701399 as active, not recruiting, with an update posted 24 June 2026. No later company release saying the program was withdrawn was used here.
