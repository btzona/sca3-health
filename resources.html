Where to go next

Resources

Organizations, the clinical reference, clinic finders, and registries that enroll families. Each link was checked when this page was added. A group’s own page is the place to confirm what they offer now.

Read the consent form. Ask what data are shared, and what you can withdraw.

National Ataxia Foundation

Patient organization for the ataxias in the United States, with an SCA3 section, a research news stream, and a pipeline page.

ataxia.org · SCA3 / MJD · SCA3 pipeline

CRC-SCA

The Clinical Research Consortium for the Study of Cerebellar Ataxia. NAF’s page says it is recruiting people in the United States and Canada with a confirmed diagnosis of SCA1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, or 10 for a natural history study, and it points to an eligibility screen and a site coordinator. It is a study of the disease’s course, not a drug trial.

ataxia.org/crc-sca

Registries families can ask about

NAF’s research page gathers ways to take part, including the CoRDS patient registry at Sanford Research and links to natural history studies. A registry records health information for research. It is not a drug trial. Read the consent before joining.

GeneReviews

The standard clinical chapter on SCA3: inheritance, repeat ranges, diagnosis, and care. Trial news is on Where things stand.

Spinocerebellar Ataxia Type 3, NBK1196

NORD

The National Organization for Rare Disorders page on Machado-Joseph disease, written for patients and families. NORD also runs the IAMRARE registry platform for rare diseases; an SCA3-specific registry was not listed there when this page was written, so the disease page is the link.

Machado-Joseph disease

ClinicalTrials.gov

The U.S. registry of clinical studies. This search covers SCA3, Machado-Joseph disease, and ATXN3, including studies that are not currently recruiting. The filtered list on this site is Open trials.

Search the registry

Ataxia clinics

NAF’s directory of neurologists and specialty clinics, including a map of Ataxia Centers of Excellence, ataxia centers, and movement-disorder centers. NAF says the broader neurologist list is not a verification of licenses. The Centers of Excellence page is the narrower list.

Clinic directory · Ataxia Centers of Excellence

ESMI

The European Spinocerebellar ataxia type 3 / Machado-Joseph disease Initiative. Its public page describes a longitudinal cohort of people with SCA3, relatives at risk, and controls, with yearly ataxia scores and samples used to prepare trials. Participation is through the consortium’s centers.

ataxia-esmi.eu

NINDS

The U.S. National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke overview of the spinocerebellar ataxias, including Machado-Joseph disease. The older disease-specific address now redirects here.

Spinocerebellar ataxias
